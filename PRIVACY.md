# Privacy Policy for Social Media Blocker

Effective date: September 25, 2026

Social Media Blocker is a Chrome extension that blocks selected social media and video streaming websites with optional timed bypasses.

## Data the extension handles

The extension may store the following information locally in Chrome extension storage:

- Whether blocking is enabled globally.
- Which supported blocked sites are enabled or disabled.
- The supported blocked site or URL you attempted to visit.
- The date and time of a site bypass.

The extension keeps up to 200 recent bypass records. Older records are removed automatically as new records are added. Previously stored reasons are discarded when the extension updates its local state.

## How the data is used

This data is used only to provide the extension's blocking, per-site settings, access-grant, and recent-bypasses history features.

## Data sharing and sales

Social Media Blocker does not sell user data. It does not send your stored settings, blocked URLs, or timestamps to the developer, advertisers, analytics providers, or any external server.

## Third-party requests

The block page loads decorative outdoor photos from Unsplash. Those image requests are made directly by your browser to Unsplash and may provide Unsplash with standard request information such as your IP address, browser user agent, and the requested image URL. The extension does not send your stored settings or bypass history to Unsplash.

## Data storage and deletion

Data is stored locally by Chrome for this extension. You can delete it by clearing the extension's storage/data in Chrome or by uninstalling the extension.
